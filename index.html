What this check can and can't tell you

It catches the problems that make a PDF unusable with a screen reader, fast. It can't judge whether alt text is meaningful, whether the reading order makes sense, whether table headers are right, or whether colors have enough contrast. Those need a person. A full check uses a validator such as PAC or veraPDF plus a manual review.
