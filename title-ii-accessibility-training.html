ADA Title II accessibility training for government staff

Training · Last reviewed November 2026 · Sources linked inline

The ADA Title II web rule (28 CFR part 35, subpart H) requires state and local governments to make web content, including posted PDFs and Office files, conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA by April 26, 2027 or April 26, 2028, depending on size. Remediating the backlog is half the job. The other half is making sure staff stop publishing new inaccessible documents. These sessions cover that half.

Courses

  • Accessible documents for staff (60 to 90 minutes): headings, lists, tables, alternative text, links, and exporting accessible PDFs from Word and PowerPoint, using your own templates.
  • Accessible agendas, minutes and public notices (60 minutes): for clerks and board staff. Meeting packets, scanned pages and notices in more than one language.
  • Checking documents before you publish (45 to 60 minutes): for reviewers and web publishers. Word's Accessibility Checker, Acrobat's checker and veraPDF, and what each one can and cannot catch.
  • Title II and WCAG 2.1 AA for managers (45 minutes): who is affected, the compliance dates, the exceptions for archived and preexisting documents, and what to prioritize.

How the sessions work

  • Role-based: each session is built for the people attending and the documents they actually produce.
  • Hands-on: participants fix real documents, preferably your own templates, during the session.
  • Screen-reader demonstration: we show how a screen reader moves through a well-structured document and a poorly structured version of the same one.
  • Live online or recorded: recordings come with captions and an accessible handout, so new staff can take the course later.

Measuring whether it worked

  • A short task before and after the session: each participant fixes a sample document, and we report the before and after pass rates.
  • A 30-day follow-up: we check a sample of documents your staff actually published after the training.

Our training comes from our remediation work. See three city documents before and after remediation and our guides to accessible agendas and minutes and Word to accessible PDF.
