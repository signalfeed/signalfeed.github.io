Accessibility statement

About · Last reviewed November 2026 · Sources linked inline

We sell accessibility data and documents, so our own site and files should be usable by everyone. This page says what we aim for, what we check, and where we know we fall short.

Our target

We aim for this website to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. We have not had an independent audit, so we don't claim conformance; we describe what we've done and fix problems when we find them.

What we do

  • Pages use real headings, landmarks, lists and data tables with header cells and captions.
  • Text and interactive elements are built to be keyboard-operable, with visible focus, and colors are chosen for contrast.
  • Charts and images have text alternatives, and the key numbers in every chart are also in the page text or a table.
  • Sample PDFs we publish are tagged and checked with veraPDF against PDF/UA-1; the veraPDF report is published next to each sample.

Known limitations

  • Our Excel workbooks use a header row, frozen panes and named sheets, but we haven't tested them with screen readers.
  • Gumroad (our checkout) and GitHub (our dataset repository) are third-party sites we don't control.
  • Some older guide pages may have issues we haven't found yet.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on this site or in one of our files doesn't work for you, email aaron.signalfeed@gmail.com with the page or file and what happened. We'll reply within two business days and, where we can, send the content in a format that works for you.
