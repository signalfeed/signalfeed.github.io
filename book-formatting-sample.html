Book formatting sample: EPUB and KDP paperback interior

Samples · Last reviewed November 2026 · Sources linked inline

SignalFeed.dev formats finished manuscripts for ebook stores and paperback print: a reflowable EPUB 3 and a print-ready interior PDF for Amazon KDP paperbacks. It's for text-based books: novels, short-story collections, memoir and nonfiction with occasional images.

The sample below is a short original story written as a formatting demonstration. It isn't client work, and the author name is a pen name made up for the sample.

Download the sample

  • The East Window, EPUB 3 (about 90 KB). EPUBCheck 5.4 reports 0 fatal errors, 0 errors and 0 warnings. It opens in Apple Books, Calibre, Thorium and most other ebook readers.
  • The East Window, 5 × 8 inch interior (PDF, 11 pages): the complete demonstration story, which is shorter than a real paperback can be under the KDP paperback guidelines, so it shows the layout only. It has mirrored margins, chapters starting on right-hand pages, page numbers in the body only, and embedded fonts.

What the ebook includes

  • A clickable table of contents and real chapter headings, so readers and screen readers can jump between chapters.
  • Scene breaks, italics and front matter (copyright, dedication) kept from the manuscript.
  • Text descriptions (alt text) for images.
  • Accessibility metadata (schema.org) that describes only what the file actually provides. The file isn't independently certified.

What the paperback interior includes

  • Trim sizes of 5 × 8, 5.5 × 8.5 or 6 × 9 inches.
  • An inside margin (gutter) set from your page count to Amazon KDP's published minimums.
  • Chapters opening on right-hand pages, with blank pages added where needed.
  • Page numbers at the foot, and none on the title and front-matter pages.
  • All fonts embedded, at the exact trim size.

How it works

  1. Send your final, edited Word file and tell us the trim size you want.
  2. We confirm the scope and price before any work starts, then format a sample chapter for your approval.
  3. We deliver the full files once every check passes: EPUBCheck, then a trim-size and font check on the PDF, then a page-by-page human review.

Not included

Cover design, illustration or picture-book layout, editing or proofreading, and uploading to your KDP account. We don't hold publishing-industry certifications. Formatting is done with automated tooling and reviewed by a person before delivery.

Ordering

To order, email us with your manuscript's word count, image count and trim size. We reply with the scope and a fixed price before any work starts. If you prefer a marketplace that holds payment until delivery, we can set the order up as an Upwork contract.
