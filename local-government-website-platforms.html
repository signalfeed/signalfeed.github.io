How we identified platforms

  • First from the site's public DNS records, which often point to the hosting platform (no visit to the site needed).
  • Otherwise from markers in the homepage itself (for example CivicPlus document-center paths), on sites whose robots.txt allows a visit.
  • Sites that block visits and don't reveal a platform in DNS stay unidentified, so platforms that show up mainly through page markers are likely undercounted. Treat the shares as indicative.
  • These are observations of public records and pages, not vendor-confirmed customer lists. We don't name or rank any city or county here.
