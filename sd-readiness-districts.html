Large school districts: Title II PDF readiness

Data · Crawled November 2026 · Method and limits below

School districts are covered by the ADA Title II web rule and are sized by the population they serve, not by enrollment. Every district below serves 50,000 people or more, so its posted PDFs, board packets included, must meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA by April 26, 2027. In November 2026 we ran automated PDF checks on the websites of 9 large districts in Wisconsin and Minnesota.

Method and limits

These are automated checks, not a legal finding and not a WCAG conformance test. In November 2026 a crawler identifying itself as SignalFeedReadinessBot visited up to 300 public HTML pages on each main website, obeying robots.txt (including crawl delays; correction, Nov 27, 2026: one site's 300-second delay was capped at 10 seconds, and the crawler now honors the full delay or skips the site) and making at most one request per second. It collected PDF links on the same website and on the site's own document storage, and analyzed up to 400 of them (a random sample where more were found). It did not log in, submit forms, or get around any bot protection; a site that blocked, challenged or rate-limited it is reported as such.

For each PDF it recorded the page count, whether the file has a tag structure (a StructTreeRoot), whether a document language and title are set, and whether sampled pages have extractable text (pages that are images only are counted as scanned). Up to 20 PDFs per site were also run through veraPDF against PDF/UA-1, the machine-checkable part of PDF accessibility.

  • Passing these checks does not make a PDF accessible. Reading order, correct tags, table headers and image descriptions need a person.
  • Not every PDF must be fixed. The rule (28 CFR 35.201) has exceptions, including archived content kept only for reference and some preexisting documents. We cannot tell from the outside which files qualify.
  • Coverage is partial: 300 pages is not the whole site, and documents in agenda and board-packet systems on other domains (for example BoardDocs, Granicus, Legistar or Diligent) were not counted.
  • “Estimated pages to remediate” adds up the pages of sampled PDFs that fail at least one basic check (no tags, no language, or scanned) and scales that to all PDF links found. Treat it as an order of magnitude.
  • Deadlines follow the rule's population tiers: April 26, 2027 for public entities of 50,000 or more, April 26, 2028 for smaller ones. School districts are sized by the population they serve, not enrollment; we used the Census Bureau's 2023 school district population estimates.
