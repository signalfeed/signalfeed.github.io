How much does PDF remediation cost? Prices from public contracts

Data · Last reviewed November 2026 · Sources linked inline

Cities, counties and school districts budgeting for the ADA Title II web rule (WCAG 2.1 Level AA by April 26, 2027 for entities of 50,000+, April 26, 2028 for smaller ones) keep asking the same question: what does it cost to make a PDF accessible? Instead of quotes or guesses, here are prices from public US government contract and bid documents: 2,348 price points in all.

What this means for a budget

  • For ordinary text documents, public per-page prices cluster around $3.50 to $6. Complex layouts, forms, STEM content and old scanned documents cost more, sometimes several times more.
  • Fillable forms are often priced as an add-on on top of the page rate, so a form-heavy library costs more than its page count suggests.
  • Rush turnaround raises prices. Scanned documents need OCR before they can be tagged.
  • Not every document needs fixing: the rule has exceptions, including for archived content and for older documents that aren't used to apply for or access a service. Counting what's in scope first can cut the bill.

Limits, stated plainly

Washington's rates are ceilings, not what agencies paid. GPO figures are all bids, not only the winners, and are a few years old. Scopes and quality checks differ between contracts. This is a reference for budgeting and for checking quotes, not a quote, an endorsement of any vendor, or legal advice.
